# Roadmap

Where co-op is headed. These are plans, not promises, and there are no
dates: each step starts when the one before it works well.

## 1. Fallout 1 (available now)

Two-player co-op for the whole game: LAN, online and one PC.
Next up are improvements, such as a marker to point things out to the other
player, being knocked out instead of game over, and giving items to each
other. See the [ideas](https://github.com/FadingTwo/fallout1-coop/issues?q=is%3Aissue+is%3Aopen+label%3Aenhancement)
and add a 👍 to the ones you want.

## 2. Fallout 2 (next)

Co-op for Fallout 2, built on
[Fallout 2 Community Edition](https://github.com/alexbatalov/fallout2-ce).
The engine is nearly the same, so most of this mod (network, server,
menus, tests) can carry over. The same setup screen will find Fallout 1 or
Fallout 2 and let you pick.

## 3. Big Fallout 2 mods (later)

Total conversions made by other teams on the Fallout 2 engine:

- **Fallout: Sonora**
- **Fallout: Nevada**
- **Olympus 2077**

They rely on sfall, an extension of the original Fallout 2 engine that
Fallout 2 CE only partly supports. So they come after Fallout 2 co-op works,
and need sfall support first. You will still need the mod itself from its
makers; nothing of theirs is included here. This project is not affiliated
with them.
