Fallout Co-op

This is a modified version of Fallout Community Edition
(https://github.com/alexbatalov/fallout1-ce) by Alexander Batalov,
distributed free of charge for non-commercial use under the Sustainable
Use License (LICENSE.md).

Changes: two-player co-op (local and over the network), a multiplayer
menu, a first-run setup screen, self-update and bug reports. See COOP.md.

You need your own copy of Fallout (GOG or Steam) to play; no game data
is included.

The setup screen's font is derived from Noto Sans Mono (SIL Open Font
License 1.1, OFL-NotoSansMono.txt).
